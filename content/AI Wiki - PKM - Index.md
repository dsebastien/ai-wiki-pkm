# AI Wiki - PKM - Index

## Overview
Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) is the practice of collecting, organizing, connecting, and retrieving information for personal and professional use. This wiki covers the core concepts, methods, tools, practitioners, and emerging trends in the PKM space — from foundational techniques like the Zettelkasten method and Building a Second Brain, to cutting-edge developments in Agentic Knowledge Management where AI agents actively participate in knowledge work.

The wiki synthesizes insights from the vault's extensive PKM notes, published articles, and web research to serve as a comprehensive reference.
