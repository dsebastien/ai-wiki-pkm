# AI Wiki - PKM - Source - YB 2026 - Structure Your Milieu

## Citation

YB, "Structure your milieu," *Engineering Agency* (Substack), January 7, 2026. URL: https://engineeringagency.substack.com/p/structure-your-milieu

## Overview

The piece that introduces YB's core framing on information environment design: **milieu** as an individual, fluid constellation of influences that must be actively curated. Referenced in YB's later Claude-Obsidian thread ("who gets access to your brain? Read their writing religiously and block everyone else out"). This essay is the foundational statement of that position.

## Core Thesis

**"You have to be ruthless about who gets access to your subconscious."** A creator's primary imperative is deliberate curation of the information environment. Not curation as decluttering; curation as active gatekeeping, because the inputs shape what the subconscious produces.

## "Structure Your Milieu" Concept

YB defines milieu as "an ever-shifting, individual configuration of information flows" — distinct from monolithic cultural concepts like "the culture" or "the discourse." Three key features:

- **Individual configuration.** Unlike shared culture, each person's milieu is unique and fluid.
- **Selective gatekeeping.** Influences need only cohere *personally*; there is no objective right set. Curation is non-negotiable.
- **Synthesis across domains.** The goal is to identify overlaps between seemingly disparate influences — YB uses Frusciante bridging Beck and Cobain as an example of inter-domain synthesis enabled by carefully chosen inputs.

## Framework Offered

YB presents a **personal influence map** categorizing thinkers who fundamentally shifted his perspective over one year, and visualizing the connections between intellectual sources. This is an artifact every practitioner can produce: a living map of who has your attention and why.

## Practical Recommendation

Establish a systematic **input → processing → output** flow through daily documentation, building toward an integrated creative system in which curated influences translate into meaningful work. The essay treats this as the precondition for everything else in knowledge work.

## Why This Matters for PKM

- **Antecedent to consumption practice.** [[AI Wiki - PKM - Information Diet|Information diet]] and [[AI Wiki - PKM - Active Reading|active reading]] presuppose the prior question: *what goes into the funnel?* Milieu curation is the answer.
- **Contrast with serendipity machines.** Milieu curation is the opposite pole from [[AI Wiki - PKM - Serendipity Machine|the serendipity machine]] framing. Reconciliation: the serendipity machine works only after milieu structuring — random exposure within a filtered pool.
- **Links to agent context.** The milieu shapes what the user writes, and what the user writes is [[AI Wiki - PKM - Context Compounds|agent context]]. Milieu curation is upstream of context quality.
- **Practical artifact.** The "personal influence map" is a specific PKM note type worth producing — similar to the OSK identity note tradition, adapted for intellectual-input tracking.
